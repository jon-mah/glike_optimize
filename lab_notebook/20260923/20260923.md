# gLike Updates

* Increased `kappa` from 10,000 to 50,000 
* Decreased iteration count from 20 to 10 for `gLike` optimizer (limitations of feasibility with increased `kappa`)
* Issue: `CMA-ES` struggles with certain boundary conditions.
